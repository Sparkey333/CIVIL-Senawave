The BEAD network design submission (CM61-BEAD-UT-VAI38 North Wasatch Front, VAI39 South Wasatch Valley) is not a plan
set. It is the network design, diagram, cost estimate and build-out timeline that NTIA (NOFO IV.D.2.c) and the Utah
Broadband Center want certified by a Professional Engineer before the first disbursement. This file records how it was
reviewed and where everything lives, so the next grant package goes faster.

## How round 2 was done

Nine independent readings of the 38-page PDF (scope and location accounting, PON engineering, optical loss and
performance, costs, timeline, certification and document form, internal consistency, civil and permits, round-1
regression), merged to 62 findings, each then tested by two more readers (does the PDF say this; is it the certifying
PE's business and how severe), then a completeness critic and a reconciliation pass. One finding was dropped (single
uplinks: Senawave's operating decision, now an exclusion). Seven were re-labelled should-fix on lead review.

Result: 25 blocking, 33 should-fix, 3 minor. Not certifiable for either project ID. Three round-1 "checked OK" items
were reopened: the four-year test (the July 18, 2030 period end is unsourced and is four years to the day after July
18, 2026), the pole-attachment lead (90 days is the approval window only, and $165 per pole against Senawave's own
$1,500), and the BSL count (517 in the package against 506 in the approved Exhibit B; the 11 difference equals the
Herriman removal count).

## Decisions

- Certify per subgrant project ID, never per location: two separately sealed PDFs, VAI38 and VAI39, each with a
  thorough-review certification under Utah Code 58-22-603, a documents schedule (revision, page count, SHA-256), a
  basis-of-review schedule with the standards used, an exclusions schedule, seal on the cover and the certification
  page, certificate-based digital signature set to no changes allowed.
- Costs are certified only as consistent with design quantities and furnished unit rates; the timeline relative to the
  start date as defined in the executed agreement; performance "if constructed as designed" with the NOFO sentence kept.
- Separate task order with Vaix, Inc. as client, fixed fee ($21,500 recommended for both projects), E&O confirmed in
  writing first, review log from day one.
- No VAI39 seal until UBC's written approval of the 17-BSL removal is in the documents schedule, or the 17 are designed
  and costed.
- The seal date is set by third parties (executed agreement with Exhibit B as amended, UBC removal approval, Rocky
  Mountain Power survey and make-ready estimate, UDOT SR-92 agreement, Bear River crossing filing, a week of latency
  measurement), not by Jesse's next draft. Earliest realistic: mid-February 2027.
