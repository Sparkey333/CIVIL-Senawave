### Part A. Round-2 review of the Senawave BEAD Network Design Submission (Draft for PE review, November 3, 2026, 38 pages)

Reviewer: Brandon Barkey, P.E. Prepared for forwarding to Jesse Montgomery. Page numbers are the PDF's own. Round 1 was dated November 3, 2026; this round re-read all 38 pages on November 6 and 7 and re-tested every round-1 item, including the items round 1 marked checked OK.

### 1. Verdict

The package cannot be certified for either project ID: it is a self-declared working draft with 16 OPEN notes, 423 of 729 awarded locations (58 percent, and 79 percent of VAI38) have no route, diagram, port table or loss row, and the cost section states on p30 that VAI39 unit rates were set so the total equals the award while VAI38 lands on its award to the cent on preliminary quantities. The award basis itself is undocumented (517 and 729 locations, $7,960,045.60 BEAD and a July 18, 2030 period end in the package against 506, 718, $8,513,235 and February 2028 and 2029 ends reported for the approved Exhibit B), the VAI39 cost table does not foot by $106,600.79, and the one thin optical margin (Cedar Fort, 2.3 dB) goes to about zero once cable length rather than route length is used. Round 3 needs the four missing area designs, the executed award documents, a per-project BSL appendix by Fabric ID, a rebuilt loss budget and a bottom-up estimate before the certification page in Part B can be signed for either CM61-BEAD-UT-VAI38 or CM61-BEAD-UT-VAI39. This round carries 25 blocking findings, 33 should-fix findings and 3 minor notes; on lead review six round-2 blocking items (F08, F26, F27, F37, F47, F48) and the permit register (F24) were re-labelled should-fix, for the reasons given against each, and F32 stays blocking.
